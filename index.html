About Dirty Delta Water

We make drinks
that taste like
heaven.

Dirty Delta Water is a women-owned specialty drink business based in Contra Costa County. We mix soda, syrups, and non-dairy creamers into unique, flavorful drinks you won't find anywhere else.

From our signature dirty sodas to custom customer creations — where you name your own drink — every cup is built to be an experience. We also give back to local schools because we're as invested in this community as you are.
